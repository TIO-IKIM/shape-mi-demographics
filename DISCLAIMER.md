# Use of Large Language Models
Large language models were used to assist with literature
search, code scaffolding, and the drafting of the manuscripts and
this repository.

Code, experiments, numerical results, and final text were
reviewed and approved by the first author. 
Every cited reference was checked against its primary source before inclusion. 
No experimental number reported in the papers is hand-entered: each is produced by the committed code and written to files
under `experiments/`, from which the figures and tables within the manuscript are generated.

The study design is made explicitely by the authors alone without any form of help.
